1. For our initial diagram, `WatchList` is represented as a separate entity in the conceptual UML diagram, but it should be modeled as a many-to-many relationship between `User` and `SpaceObject`. In the new diagram, it is modified to a many-to-many relationship with `AddedAt` and `Note` as relationship attributes. 
2. Our initial diagram has foreign key attributes that duplicate the associations already drawn between classes. In the new diagram, these attributes are removed, and the references are shown only by associations. The foreign keys now appear only in the logical schema. We remove attributes already covered by existing associations such as `SpaceObject.Launch_Tag`, `SpaceObject.Owner`, `Launch.Launch_Site`, `Launch.Launch_Agency`, `LaunchSite.Parent`, and `Organization.Parent`. We also remove `SpaceObject.Parent`, `SpaceObject.Manufacturer`, `Organization.StateCode`, and `LaunchSite.StateCode`, and add new associations including "parent of", "manufactures", and "state of" for them.
